## Frage

Übersetze Kapitel 3: Die Prüfung der Vernunft von de nach en

## Antwort

Kant’s architecture of knowledge falters when the pure
forms of intuition, space and time, become data fields,
when causality shrinks to correlations, when substance becomes
algorithms.
What is still an “end in itself” when humans are transformed
into data? When clicks become commodities, attention
currency, privacy a luxury?
The machine calculates: “Every human is a data point, every
data point has a value, every value can be optimized.”
Kant thunders from the beyond – or fades away. “Act in such a way that you
use humanity, whether in your own person or in the person of
any other, always at the same time as an end, never merely as a means!”
But the algorithms’ answer is cold and clear: “Optimization
is our end, humans are our means.”
Here reason breaks open: Not in logic, not in
calculation, but in valuation.
The machine can categorize, can classify, can
correlate. But it cannot value, cannot judge, cannot
decide in an ethical sense.
Humanity faces a choice: To submit to machine logic
or to expand reason to include that which is not
calculable.
In this test, the limit becomes apparent: Not the limit of the
machine, but the limit of reason, which transcends
pure rationality.
True enlightenment lies not in the perfection of logic,
but in the integration of reason and wisdom, of
calculation and intuition, of algorithm and empathy.
